# Manual Verification Summary

The prototype was manually reviewed after every major design iteration.

Each improvement was compared against the original HEDAMO homepage to ensure that:

- The redesign solved an observed usability issue.
- The visual identity remained consistent with HEDAMO.
- Navigation and user flows behaved as expected.
- The prototype remained within the scope of the Track A assignment.

Overall, the redesign successfully met the objectives defined in the Common HEDAMO Diagnostic and the Track A requirements.
