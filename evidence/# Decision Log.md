# Decision Log

This document explains the key product and design decisions made during the HEDAMO Track A assignment, the alternatives considered, and the rationale behind the final approach.

---

# Decision 1

## Decision

Improve the homepage by reorganising the information hierarchy instead of redesigning the entire visual identity.

## Alternatives Considered

### Option A

Completely redesign the homepage using a modern SaaS style with new colours, illustrations, and layouts.

### Option B (Chosen)

Preserve HEDAMO's existing editorial design language while improving first-time comprehension through better information hierarchy, clearer messaging, and improved call-to-actions.

## Why This Approach

The existing HEDAMO homepage already communicates credibility and aligns with the company's research-oriented positioning.

The primary challenge was not visual design but helping first-time visitors understand the platform more quickly.

Improving clarity while preserving the brand identity creates less disruption and better supports existing users.

# Decision 2

## Decision

Design a transparent producer application status flow instead of a traditional dashboard.

## Alternatives Considered

### Option A

Display application statuses as simple badges within a table.

### Option B (Chosen)

Create a visual status timeline showing:

Submitted

↓

Needs Information

↓

Under Review

↓

Approved

or

↓

Not Proceeding

## Why This Approach

A timeline provides users with a clearer understanding of their application's current stage and next possible outcome.

This approach reduces uncertainty and improves transparency throughout the review process.

---

# Decision 3

## Decision

Prioritise improvements using an Impact vs. Effort framework.

## Alternatives Considered

### Option A

List all observed issues without prioritisation.

### Option B

Prioritise based only on visual improvements.

### Option C (Chosen)

Prioritise improvements according to expected user impact and implementation effort.

## Why This Approach

This method reflects how product teams typically make roadmap decisions.

It ensures that improvements delivering the greatest user value are addressed first while remaining practical to implement.

---

# Summary

The overall approach focused on improving usability, transparency, and first-time comprehension while respecting HEDAMO's philosophy, visual identity, and research-driven positioning.

Rather than redesigning the product, the objective was to refine the existing experience through evidence-based product thinking.
